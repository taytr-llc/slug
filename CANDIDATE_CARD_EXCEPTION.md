candidate, not an amendment to D13, D28 or D52. Pair only opposing gyms'
fighters in the same weight class whose noon-cutoff D65 ratings are within

with a reason for each unmatched fighter. Counts and reasons are evidence for
later thin-league policy; they do not establish a production rule. The current
scope has at most one opposing entrant per class, so an eligible pair has no
alternative opponent to draw. Do not claim a meaningful random draw or a
general multi-gym matching algorithm from these cards.

priority remain unresolved. The zero-record candidate profile must remain
explicit and separate from any future authoritative professional results.

no official ownership, results, awards, training, XP or title authority.
