# Selected own-wallet wire reference

This source-only producer will cover the narrow additive WIRE2 family after its
ordered contract is frozen by the desktop author and selected by root. It will
not change or execute the first WIRE1 stage. Root alone executes a separately
approved lock and compares two interpreter outputs.

Use the qualified DAILY WIRE1 selection and the unchanged Set67/Set70 wire
corpora. Reuse their conditional transaction/payment payloads and canonical
amount examples; do not regenerate transactions, wallet observations, account
authority, allocation inventories or pool economics. The new derivation is
limited to explicit WIRE2 envelopes, original selected bindings, operation and
capture correlation, local page positions, decimal amount spellings and finite
frame bounds. Rebinding a historical payload to the selected public View is a
conditional codec example, not evidence that this wallet owns that transaction.

The smallest case families are:

- Exact selected activity begin/job/cancel, empty/one/eight/tail/end pages and
  retained detail; working/failed/cancelled refresh preserves a distinctly
  identified older capture. A new successful capture invalidates old selectors.
- Inherited incoming, outgoing, self-payment and unknown-fee payloads; zero,
  negative net, maximum supported amount and canonical decimal refusals. No
  amount is reconstructed by adding historical activity to a current balance.
- Exact selected payout observation for not-observed, unconfirmed, confirmed
  and unavailable; derive payment atoms/script from the retained DAILY row.
  Identical First/Current copies represent one tuple; conflicting copies,
  foreign capture/batch/tx/output, evicted membership and replaced capture fail.
- Required ordered fields/null arms, one LF, fixed command/result-arm mapping,
  monotonic request IDs, original View/full binding, exact operation/capture
  selectors, decimal overflow/spelling and arm-specific frame ceilings.

Expected runtime facts stay declarative: caller timeout after admission retains
the original operation; cancellation/Stop/Close join; old View or replaced
capture cannot publish; no original account lease spans wallet/DB/RPC work.
The producer cannot prove those facts and will not label a modeled transition as
an executed owner/lifetime test.

Do not copy the complete core transaction/storage reference families. Their
qualified wire payloads are enough for this transport slice. Current named
inputs include the original payload/type/codec declarations and the missing
allocation2 dispatch source solely as provenance. A final catalog and complete
source lock will replace the pending catalog only after the exact new contract
is supplied. No expected JSON exists or may be generated in this lane.
