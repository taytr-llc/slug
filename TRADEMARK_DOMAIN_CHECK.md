# Brawlchain names: preliminary trademark and domain knock-out check

Run November 14, 2026 (PT). Public-source knock-out search only. Not legal advice. Nothing was registered, bought, reserved, or contacted.

## Brawlchain: LIKELY CONFLICT

- **Block Brawlers**, an on-chain NFT fighting game by GAME Credits running on SKALE, calls its chain **"BRAWLCHAIN"** (SKALE partner spotlight: "Block Brawlers' BRAWLCHAIN, running on SKALE"). Same name, same field: blockchain fighting game with tradable fighter NFTs and a token.
- **BLOCKCHAIN BRAWLERS** is a live federal registration (Serial 97264012, Verona Holdings SEZC) covering online blockchain games, NFT marketplaces, and earnable tokens (Classes 35, 41, 42).
- **BRAWL** is registered by Supercell Oy (Reg. 6914997) for game software and entertainment.
- No exact BRAWLCHAIN USPTO record surfaced in the public mirrors searched. Absence of a find, not proof none exists.
- Domains: brawlchain.com is registered (created 2024-10-22, Unstoppable Domains). brawlchain.io and brawlchain.gg appear available (registry RDAP endpoints unreachable; based on rdap.org 404 plus no web presence; re-verify at a registrar).

Biggest concern: an existing blockchain fighting game already uses BRAWLCHAIN as its chain name, and the near-identical BLOCKCHAIN BRAWLERS mark is registered for exactly this kind of product.

## KOIN: LIKELY CONFLICT (for a tradable token)

- **Koinos blockchain's native token is KOIN.** It trades on exchanges under the ticker KOIN (Coinbase lists "Koinos (KOIN)").
- **Koinos Group LLC holds a live US registration for KOIN** (Reg. 6800011, July 26, 2022, Class 36) covering cryptocurrency/financial database services. Their token-specific application (Serial 90291773) was abandoned in 2021, but the Class 36 registration lives.
- **KOIN Mobile, LLC** has a pending application (Serial 98890638) for KOIN covering electronic payment services for casino gaming credits.
- **KOIN (channel 6)** is the CBS affiliate in Portland, Oregon (Nexstar Media Group, on air since 1953, branded "KOIN 6"). koin.com has been registered since 1995.
- **Koin** is also a well-known Kotlin dependency injection library (insert-koin.io), and **Koin Games** is a web3 game studio.
- Domains: koin.com registered since 1995. getkoin.com registered 2018, parked and listed for sale (Afternic). koin.gg appears available (same .gg caveat as above).

Biggest concern: the ticker and name are already occupied by a live, exchange-traded token in the identical field, backed by a live federal registration. A second tradable KOIN invites exchange confusion, misdirected funds, and a strong infringement claim.

## The Mine Event: USABLE WITH CAUTION

- No exact-match brand, event, or game feature named "The Mine Event" was found, and no USPTO registration or application for MINE EVENT or THE MINE EVENT surfaced.
- "Mine Event" is used descriptively inside other games (Eatventure, MU Online) as a feature label, and mining-industry conferences use similar names (The Electric Mine, THE Mining Investment Event). Different fields, but the phrase is generic and search results will be noisy.
- Domains: themineevent.com appears available (Verisign RDAP 404, which for .com means unregistered). mineevent.com is registered (2020) but parked and listed for sale on Atom.

Biggest concern: weakness, not a blocking owner. The phrase may be hard to protect or enforce as a mark because it is descriptive.

## SLUG: USABLE WITH CAUTION (checked Nov 15, 2026, at Mike's pick)

- Crypto: the ticker is used by two negligible meme tokens, Unlucky Slug (Ethereum, listed on CoinMarketCap, effectively dead, market cap about $0) and SlugLord (market cap about $2,700, zero volume). No living project owns it, but ticker searches will collide with them.
- Trademark: no live exact SLUG registration in Classes 9, 36, 41, or 42 covering software, crypto/financial services, or online gaming was found. The only live exact SLUG registration is for industrial chemical mixing machinery. Older exact marks (toys, footwear, drain cleaner) are dead.
- **METAL SLUG (SNK) is the dominant gaming use of the word.** The franchise is actively maintained and licensed in 2026: METAL SLUG ATTACK and METAL SLUG COMMANDER registrations are live and renewed, with 30th anniversary merchandise on sale. One mirror reports the headline METAL SLUG registration (Reg. 4599364) was cancelled in March 2026 while another mirror shows it maintained; the mirrors conflict and a live USPTO check is needed before relying on either. The family registrations and continuous use keep the brand alive regardless. Plain SLUG does not copy the composite mark, but a SLUG game brand will fight Metal Slug for search and mindshare, with some risk SNK argues association.
- Domains: all taken. slug.com (1998, parked pattern), getslug.com (2021, appears genuinely held), slugcoin.com (2024, portfolio registrar, likely held for resale), slug.io and slug.gg registered.

Biggest concern: SNK's actively maintained METAL SLUG family in gaming, plus zero available natural domains. The word itself is strong: distinctive, not generic for fighting, with a true triple meaning (a punch, a lump of metal, mining-camp money).

## FITENITE: LIKELY CONFLICT (checked Nov 15, 2026, Mike's proposal; coin name as brand name)

- Crypto: clean. No token named FITENITE or FITE NITE and no FITENITE ticker on the listings searched.
- **EA's FIGHT NIGHT is the blocker.** FITENITE is pronounced exactly as "fight night," and trademark judges marks substantially by sound, so the respelling buys no distance. EA announced the Fight Night franchise's return on Oct 20, 2026 (pre-production, EA's simulation fighting team) and filed fresh US applications for EA SPORTS FIGHT NIGHT on Oct 23, 2026 in Class 9 (game software) and Class 41 (online game services), the exact classes a blockchain fight league game needs. The franchise ran five games, 2004 to 2011.
- UFC FIGHT NIGHT (Zuffa) is a live, renewed federal registration in Class 41, and "fight night" is the generic label for an evening of fights, so the phrase is descriptive of the product itself and would be a weak mark even if registered.
- The exact name is already used in boxing: FITE NITE, a Texas amateur boxing event sanctioned by USA Boxing's South Texas association, owns fitenite.com (registered 2001) with event history back to the 1980s. Pinnacle FiteNite ran as a pro boxing PPV series in 2007. No exact FITENITE USPTO filing was found, but common law use predates the project by decades.
- FITE (now TrillerTV, "powered by FITE," fite.tv) is a decade-old combat sports streaming brand; no live US FITE registration in entertainment surfaced, so its position is common law.
- Domains: fitenite.com registered and in active use by the Texas event; fitenite.io and fitenite.gg unverifiable from the check environment.

Verdict: likely conflict, the worst result of any name checked. As the overall brand for a fighting game built around a weekly fight night, it stacks EA's revived franchise, a generic phrase, and prior exact-name use in boxing. Mike's underlying point stands on its own: the coin name should be the brand name. FITENITE is just the wrong word to do it with.

## RUMBLE: LIKELY CONFLICT (checked Nov 15, 2026, at Mike's pick)

- **Rumble Inc. is the blocker, and it is now a crypto company under this name.** The video platform (Nasdaq: RUM, roughly 85 US trademark filings) launched Rumble Wallet with Tether in January 2026 (self-custodial, BTC/USDT/XAUT, a Finance-category app in both app stores), took a $775M Tether investment, runs a Bitcoin treasury, and has a live Class 9 application for RUMBLE WALLET covering cryptocurrency trading and tipping software, plus a second crypto app filing (FREEDOM-FIRST). Its core RUMBLE registration (Reg. 4986133, Class 42) was renewed in November 2025, and RUMBLE SPORTS is registered in Classes 38/41 for sports broadcasting and entertainment. No Class 36 registration for RUMBLE itself was found, but a coin is marketed through apps and exchanges, which is exactly the Class 9/42 ground they are staking out. An unaffiliated token already markets itself as "inspired by the popular platform Rumble," showing the confusion exists in embryo.
- Fighting and gaming pile-on: WWE's ROYAL RUMBLE portfolio is live and renewed; RUMBLE BOXING (Reg. 7600026, Class 41) is a registered boxing gym franchise brand, awkward given the project's gym framing; Rumble Kong League is a live web3 sports game; Blizzard sells Warcraft Rumble; Rumbleverse (Epic/Iron Galaxy) is defunct. A bare RUMBLE application for a combat sports matchmaking app was abandoned in 2020.
- Crypto: the exact RUMBLE ticker is squatted by Realm Rumble (about $0, zero volume); a Rumble (RUM) token and a dead Rumble Gaming (RMBL) token also exist.
- Domains: all registered. rumble.com (1998, Rumble Inc.), getrumble.com (2016), rumble.io (2011, in use), rumble.gg (parked for sale).

Verdict: likely conflict. A tradable coin named RUMBLE would predictably be mistaken for Rumble Inc.'s own token, and that owner actively files, renews, and has the resources to enforce.

## SLUGFEST: LIKELY CONFLICT for company + coin (checked Nov 15, 2026, at Mike's request)

- **SlugFest Games West, LLC (Oakland, CA) is the blocker.** A games company using SLUGFEST since the 2000s (The Red Dragon Inn line), with a live application for SLUGFEST GAMES (Serial 99277206, Notice of Allowance June 2026) covering downloadable video game software in Class 9 and board games in Class 28. It is currently in an active two-way TTAB dispute over the word with another applicant (John Duck, Serial 99207431), with both sides filing opposition extensions in late 2025 and early 2026 and settlement talks noted. Demonstrated willingness to spend on defending the word.
- **Tennessee Slugfest** is a live combat sports promotion founded in 2026 (Black Flag Sports & Entertainment LLC): sold-out cage boxing and MMA events, PPV, merchandise, about 95K Facebook followers, at slugfestfights.com. Exact word, exact business, no registration needed for common law problems.
- Historical gaming marks are dead: Midway's MLB SlugFest registration died in 2009, and Williams' 1992 SlugFest pinball registration was cancelled in 2003. Neither explains the slugfest.com holding; the domain's current owner remains undisclosed and no for-sale listing exists.
- Other live records: SLUGFEST for fireworks (Phantom I.P., Notice of Allowance Oct 2025) and SLUG-FEST for slug pesticide (ORCAL, renewed 2022), both unrelated fields. A SLUG FEST PROMOTIONS registration for combat sports promotion services (2013, Principal Register) was cancelled in 2019.
- Genericity: the USPTO has repeatedly placed SLUGFEST on the Principal Register, so the word is registrable where it evokes rather than describes; the weakness is crowding, not descriptiveness. A new SLUGFEST mark would be narrow and exposed to opposition from the games incumbent.
- Crypto: empty except one dead pump.fun token named SLUGFEST with no market. No Class 36 or Class 42 SLUGFEST mark surfaced.
- Domains: slugfest.gg, slugfest.io, slugfest.app, and slugfest.club confirmed open; slugfest.com held and not for sale.

Verdict: as a coin alone it might have been usable with caution; as the company name and coin for a fight game, it walks into a games incumbent mid-litigation over the word plus a live fight promotion using it. Not adopted unless Mike overrides.

## SLUGGER and Slugger Night (checked Nov 15, 2026, at Mike's pick)

- **SLUGGER as coin and brand: likely conflict.** The blocker is Wilson Sporting Goods, owner of the Louisville Slugger brand since 2015. Wilson owns the standalone word SLUGGER in six US registrations (including Reg. 3619985 for clothing, Reg. 5794764 for helmets, bags, apparel, and sporting goods, and Reg. 6329137 covering retail services plus Class 41 sports training, arranging and conducting athletic competitions, and entertainment services for athletic events). The LOUISVILLE SLUGGER family dates to an 1894 trademark and dozens of registrations. Wilson enforces: in Wilson v. Moore (TTAB, decided Sep 27, 2024) it won an opposition against SLUGG'N for graphic t-shirts, pleading 32 registrations and "exceptionally strong common law rights" in SLUGGER, and both key SLUGGER registrations carry a 2022 Notice of Suit. No SLUGGER mark in Class 36 (crypto/financial) or Class 42 surfaced, and no SLUGGER token exists on CoinGecko, CoinMarketCap, or DexScreener, but the empty crypto classes do not cure a famous sports mark with Class 41 competition coverage and an enforcing owner. A game company (Neowiz) filed SLUGGER in Class 9 in 2013 and expressly abandoned it in 2014. In fighting, a Prague boxing and MMA gym trades as Slugger Fight Club and runs amateur "Open Ring" nights (common law, small). slugger.com is Louisville Slugger's official site (registered 1995); getslugger.com belongs to an unrelated baseball walk-up-song app; slugger.gg appears available.
- **Slugger Night as event name: likely conflict as a brand asset.** The exact phrase is unoccupied: no USPTO record for SLUGGER NIGHT surfaced, no event uses the exact name, and sluggernight.com (Verisign 404) and sluggernight.gg both appear available. But it is Wilson's registered word plus a descriptive word, for the same service Wilson's Class 41 registration covers (athletic events and sports competitions), in front of an owner that sued over SLUGG'N. Separately, "Silver Slugger Night" is an established minor-league baseball promotion (senior fan nights at Stockton, Worcester, West Michigan, St. Lucie), so the phrase space already reads baseball. Workable only as a throwaway descriptive label, not a defensible brand.
